About Me

I am an applied mathematics researcher specializing in Scientific Machine Learning (SciML), partial differential equations (PDEs), and hybrid optimization mechanics. I completed my Master of Science in Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra, graduating as the Institute Gold Medalist (CGPA 9.54/10.0).

My research focuses on bridging rigorous analytical theory with modern data-driven computation. During my Master's dissertation under the supervision of Dr. Raghvendra Pratap Singh, I investigated foundational optimization pathologies in Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs). To overcome gradient stiffness, ill-conditioned loss landscapes, and premature convergence in non-convex regimes, I designed hybrid optimization frameworks combining population-based global heuristics (Particle Swarm Optimization) with local gradient descent for elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic systems.

My subsequent work has extended into high-dimensional nonlinear dynamics (3D & 5D Burgers' and Navier–Stokes systems) and boundary-layer asymptotics in singularly perturbed differential-difference systems via exponential fitting schemes. Looking forward, I aim to develop mathematically grounded, provably stable, and structure-preserving Neural Operators (FNOs, DeepONets) and Reduced-Order Models (ROMs) for multiscale dynamical systems.
