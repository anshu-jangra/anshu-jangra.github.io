Hello, I am

Anshu

Mathematics Researcher · Applied Mathematics & Scientific Machine Learning

I am a Mathematics researcher specializing in Scientific Machine Learning (SciML), partial differential equations (PDEs), and hybrid optimization mechanics. I obtained my Master of Science in Mathematics from the National Institute of Technology, Kurukshetra (2023–2025), graduating as the Institute Gold Medalist (CGPA 9.54/10.0), mentored by Dr. Raghvendra Pratap Singh. Previously, I completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honours) at the University of Delhi (2020–2023) with First Division (CGPA 9.32). I am actively looking for a Ph.D. position in Applied Mathematics, Scientific Computing, and Operator Learning.

My research trajectory is driven by the goal of moving beyond empirical black-box machine learning to establish rigorous mathematical foundations, optimization mechanics, and generalization guarantees for neural differential equation solvers. In my Master’s research, I investigated gradient pathologies, stiffness, and non-convex loss landscapes in Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), developing hybrid optimization frameworks that combine global heuristics (Particle Swarm Optimization) with gradient-based local refinement to guarantee reliable convergence. Following this, I extended computational frameworks to high-dimensional systems (3D & 5D Burgers' and Navier–Stokes equations) and boundary-layer phenomena in singularly perturbed differential-difference equations via exponential-fitting schemes.

In my doctoral research, I aim to investigate Neural Operators (FNOs, DeepONets) and Reduced-Order Modeling (ROM) from a rigorous mathematical standpoint: developing approximation and generalization bounds for singular multiscale systems, structure-preserving optimization techniques that respect conservation laws, and robust methods for ill-posed inverse problems.

Research Interests: Scientific Machine Learning (SciML); Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs); Neural Operators & DeepONets; Partial Differential Equations (Nonlinear, 3D & 5D Burgers' / Navier–Stokes); Hybrid Optimization Dynamics; Loss Landscape Mechanics; Singular Perturbations & Boundary Layer Asymptotics; Structure Preservation & Inverse Problems.

If any of my research excites you, or if you would like to discuss computational mathematics and data-driven physics, I am always open to discussions and collaborations. When I step away from research, you can find me exploring global cultures, trying international cuisines, or stargazing and pondering the limits of mathematical systems.

Research Focus

Scientific Machine Learning & PINNs

Investigating the training dynamics, spectral bias, and optimization landscapes of neural networks applied to forward and inverse differential systems.

Operator Learning & ROM

Analyzing approximation theory, stability, and generalization error bounds for Fourier Neural Operators (FNOs) and DeepONets across parameter regimes.

High-Dimensional & Nonlinear PDEs

Computational treatments and surrogate modeling of multiscale 3D/5D Burgers' equations, Navier–Stokes dynamics, and singularly perturbed boundary layers.

Hybrid Optimization Mechanics

Coupling global metaheuristics (Particle Swarm Optimization) with gradient-based optimizers (ADAM / L-BFGS) to escape bad local minima and gradient stagnation.

Structure-Preserving Learning

Designing learning algorithms that respect underlying physical invariants, variational principles, and fundamental conservation laws.

Numerical Analysis & Asymptotics

Developing uniformly convergent exponential fitting schemes for stiff differential-difference equations exhibiting sharp boundary layers.
