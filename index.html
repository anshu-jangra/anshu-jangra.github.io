About Me

Hello, I am Anshu. From August 2023 to July 2025, I completed my Master of Science in Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology, Kurukshetra, where I graduated as the Institute Gold Medalist (CGPA 9.5422/10.0) under the supervision of Dr. Raghvendra Pratap Singh. Prior to this, I completed my Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Mathematics at the University of Delhi (2020–2023) with First Division (CGPA 9.324).

My long-term goal is to design efficient optimization strategies and robust scientific machine learning frameworks for solving complex, high-dimensional, and nonlinear partial differential equations (PDEs). I am particularly interested in analyzing non-convex loss landscapes, overcoming gradient stiffness, and advancing the expressivity and explainability of neural differential equation solvers to ensure they remain mathematically rigorous, interpretable, and structure-preserving.

During my Master’s research, I investigated the training mechanics of Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), developing hybrid optimization techniques that combine global exploration (Particle Swarm Optimization) with gradient-based local refinement to resolve training pathologies. Building on this, I applied frameworks to challenging systems, including 3D & 5D Burgers' and Navier–Stokes equations as well as singularly perturbed differential-difference equations.

Looking ahead, I want to explore the expressivity and mathematical explainability of Neural Networks (NNs) and Neural Operators (NOs), analyzing their representation capacity, error bounds, and transparency when learning infinite-dimensional mappings in complex physical systems.

I am actively looking for a Ph.D. position in Applied Mathematics, Scientific Machine Learning, and Operator Learning.
