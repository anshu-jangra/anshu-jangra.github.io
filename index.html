About Me

About

During my Master's research at the National Institute of Technology Kurukshetra, I investigated optimization strategies for training Physics-Informed Neural Networks to solve partial differential equations.

My subsequent research has involved high-dimensional PDEs, including 3D and 5D Burgers' and Navier–Stokes equations, as well as numerical methods for differential-difference equations.
