About Me

About

I am a Mathematics researcher with an academic background in Mathematics and a strong interest in computational and data-driven approaches to mathematical problems.

My research interests include efficient optimization strategies, deep learning, scientific machine learning, and partial differential equations.

My Master's research focused on training Physics-Informed Neural Networks using hybrid optimization techniques. I investigated Particle Swarm Optimization combined with gradient-based optimization for solving different classes of partial differential equations.
