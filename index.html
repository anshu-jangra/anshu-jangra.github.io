Hi, I am a Mathematics researcher specializing in Scientific Machine Learning (SciML), partial differential equations (PDEs), and hybrid optimization mechanics. I completed my Master of Science in Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology, Kurukshetra (2023–2025), where I was awarded the Institute Gold Medal (CGPA 9.54/10.0), under the supervision of Dr. Raghvendra Pratap Singh.

Prior to this, I completed my undergraduate studies with a B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi in 2023 with First Division (CGPA 9.32).

My primary research interests lie in the theoretical and computational aspects of Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), Neural Operators (FNOs, DeepONets), and hybrid optimization strategies that couple global metaheuristics with gradient-based methods to overcome non-convex loss landscapes. Additionally, I work on high-dimensional systems (3D & 5D Burgers' and Navier–Stokes equations) and boundary-layer phenomena in singularly perturbed differential-difference equations.

I am actively seeking Ph.D. positions in Applied Mathematics, Scientific Computing, and Operator Learning starting in 2025/2026.

Publications & Preprints

  • A Hybrid Particle Swarm and Gradient-Based Optimization Technique for Training Physics-Informed Neural Networks to Solve Partial Differential Equations

    Anshu, Raghvendra Pratap Singh  ·  Under Review

    Investigates gradient pathologies and bad local minima in PINN training, introducing a coupled PSO and gradient-descent scheme for elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic PDEs.

  • A Numerical Scheme Utilizing Exponential Fitting to Solve a Singularly Perturbed Differential-Difference Equation Featuring a Boundary Layer Phenomenon

    Anshu, et al.  ·  Under Review

    Develops a uniformly convergent exponential-fitting numerical scheme to accurately resolve boundary and interior layers in parameter-dependent delay differential equations.
